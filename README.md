# No Game No Life Zero
*No Game No Life: Zero* is a Japanese animated film based on The Gamer Couple Who Challenged the World!, the sixth volume of the light novel series *No Game No Life* by Yuu Kamiya.

*No Game No Life* is a light novel series that have an anime adaptation were the premise of the series revolves around the world of *Disboard* and the adventures of the gamers: Sora and Shiro. In this world, evrything is decided via games. Such as the fate of a person, war, politics, and even their economy.

The movies narrates a story from 6,000 years ago, during the Great War.
It tells the story of Riku and Shuvi, who, during the Great War, strive to end the conflict by taking hold of the *Suniaster*, a conceptuak device that bends reality to the will of the wielder. But before Riku can take hold of the Suniaster, his body started disintegrating, and an *Old Deus* step up and took hold. The Old Deus' name is Tet, the God of Play, and remade the world inspired by the chess games he played with Riku.
